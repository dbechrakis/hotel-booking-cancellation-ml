# Licensing scope

The following code is released under the MIT license in the root `LICENSE`:

- the decision-system package under `src/hotel_cancellation/`;
- the Streamlit application in `app/` and the `app.py` entry point;
- the training, export and download scripts under `scripts/`;
- the tests under `tests/` and the evidence checks under `ci/`;
- the GitHub Actions workflow in `.github/workflows/evidence.yml` and the `Makefile`.

The source data and everything derived from it (the exported model artifact in `artifacts/`, the holdout predictions and metrics in `outputs/`, and the figures) remain subject to the Hotel Booking Demand dataset's own terms, not this code license.

This is not a blanket relicensing of the repository. The notebook, the archived office documents in `docs/`, original coursework and third-party dependencies remain subject to their existing rights and terms. No additional redistribution permission for those materials is asserted here.
